## Geographic scope

Clark County's park locator lists Spring Valley Community Park at **7600 West Flamingo, Las Vegas, NV 89147**, and assigns it to the Spring Valley community. County planning identifies Spring Valley as an unincorporated town in the western Las Vegas Valley administered by Clark County. This supports **Spring Valley / Las Vegas Valley, Clark County, and Nevada** as an initial coverage area; it does not establish that every address in the ZIP lies in Spring Valley or inside the incorporated City of Las Vegas. Keep area filters editable and use City of Las Vegas material as neighboring metro coverage. No exact residential address is needed. [County park location](https://parkslocator.clarkcountynv.gov/Search/ParkDetail?parkId=79), [county Spring Valley planning page](https://www.clarkcountynv.gov/government/departments/comprehensive_planning_department/divisions/advanced_planning_division/spring-valley).

## Collection contract and unresolved checks

The source observations above establish candidates, not working feeds. Before promising a local block, validate discovery, item/detail fetching, date parsing, deduplication, pagination, and permitted automated collection for the selected source. If dynamic pages cannot be collected reliably, use another explicitly selected public source or show local coverage unavailable. Avoid bypassing access restrictions. No complete local-news or event coverage is established.

Product recommendation: retain article publication time separately from fetch time; a new fetch must not make an old notice a fresh headline. Distinguish an event's occurrence date from its announcement date or booking deadline. Normalize explicitly local timed events to `America/Los_Angeles` with daylight-saving handling; preserve any supplied timezone and label ambiguous times rather than guessing. Keep date-only events date-only. Expand recurring events only when occurrences are supported by the source; preserve ongoing date ranges, cancellation/postponement and registration-required status. Recheck near-term listings and keep organizer links so changed availability can be checked. These are proposed adapter behaviors, not tested source guarantees.

Scope official releases as **civic announcements** and library listings as **community events**. Independent local reporting and broader commercial concerts/sports would require additional publisher/organizer selection. No broad automated reuse license was established in this review; public visibility alone is not blanket republication permission. The city, state and library pages carry copyright notices. Start with concise factual fields, original source links and attribution; review each selected site's collection/reuse terms before a deployed collector or public examples. No recurring paid-source commitment is implied.

When collection succeeds but finds nothing in the configured window, say no new items; when it fails, say unavailable. Any cached listing keeps its original as-of time and stale label. Do not describe a past event as upcoming, invent venue/time/admission information, or fill a news slot with an unsupported AI claim.
