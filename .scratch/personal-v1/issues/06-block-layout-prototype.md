## Question

What is the simplest block-based configuration and receipt-preview experience that meets the user's layout needs at the chosen paper width? Use a throwaway prototype to decide block ordering, per-block options, typography, length limits, and whether multiple named layouts are necessary. Compare an ordered block editor with a free-positioned canvas only as far as needed for the user's reaction.
