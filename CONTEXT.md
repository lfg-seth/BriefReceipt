# Receipt Summary

A personal morning briefing at home, combining personal and work information on a printed receipt.

## Language

**Briefing**:
A collected edition of information prepared for the reader's day.
_Avoid_: Summary, digest, report when referring to the complete edition.

**Block**:
A configurable section of a briefing, such as weather, calendar events, news, or project updates.

**Layout**:
The arrangement and presentation of blocks in a briefing.

**Project**:
A selected unit of work identified by a configured local folder. Its briefing updates combine available repository activity and explicitly dated local work context.

**Market snapshot**:
A collection of observations for tracked market instruments at a point in time, with the observation time attached to each value.
